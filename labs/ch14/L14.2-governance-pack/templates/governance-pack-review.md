# Governance pack review

Copy to `evidence/governance-pack-review.md`. The reviewer is a person accountable for the area, not the author.

## 1. Scope and notice

What the pack covers, as of which date, and the sentence that says it is not a compliance claim.

## 2. Permission matrix

The draft's findings and what each would have allowed. Who holds each two-person permission.

## 3. DPIA

Each draft finding and its fix. The data protection officer's consultation date and the next review date. Any flow
whose lawful basis or transfer mechanism the DPO has not yet confirmed.

## 4. Licences

The denied and reviewed components, the decision for each, and who made it. How generated code's provenance is
recorded.

## 5. AI Act

Each feature's tier and the provision behind it. The date you checked the application dates, and the source. What
a qualified person must still confirm.

## 6. Frameworks

For NIST SSDF, the NIST AI RMF and ISO/IEC 42001: which practices or clauses the pack's evidence maps to, and which
have no evidence yet. Write "maps to", never "complies with".

## 7. What an agent produced

Which parts of this pack an agent drafted, and how each was checked.
