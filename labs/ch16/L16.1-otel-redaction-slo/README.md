## Brief

On Tuesday 2 February 2027 Relay's latency objective paged on time, an incident assistant blamed the 15:00 deploy,
and a rollback fixed nothing while a credential-stuffing attack continued. Chapter 16 tells the story (a composite).

You have what Relay built afterwards: **Relay 4.27.0** in `relay-repo/`. Its new package `src/relay/telemetry/`
records traces, metrics and logs in OpenTelemetry's data model and writes them as OTLP JSON for a collector. It
allowlists attribute keys and redacts values in layers, evaluates SLOs with the Site Reliability Workbook's
multi-window, multi-burn-rate policy, runs runtime fitness checks, deploys Chapter 14's detection rules, and routes
alerts to pages. `docs/slo/` holds the SLOs, the budget policy and the D7 pilot review.

You will build the checks around it: a redaction probe harness, sliding windows, the burn-rate policy and the
scoring of alert episodes, error budgets with policy actions, and ESC-AC30 per pilot week.

**About the data.** Everything in `data/` is **generated** by `scripts/generate_data.py`, deterministically, and
labelled as such. The 30 days of API traffic, the AI-quality labels, the pilot month and the agent journal are
synthetic, and the incidents in them were configured in the generator: the alert results show what each strategy
does with those patterns, not how often a real service fails. The token in the data is fake.

## What you will practise

- **Minimising** telemetry by design, and **probing** a redactor for bypasses.
- Turning a per-minute series into **SLIs, burn rates and alert episodes**, and judging alerts by pages, false
  pages, detection time and reset time.
- **Error budgets** and a budget policy.
- **Runtime fitness checks** and a decision made from pilot evidence.

## How the tests choose what to grade

Tests run against `starter/` by default. To check the reference, in bash: `LAB_TARGET=solution python -m pytest -q`.
In PowerShell: `$env:LAB_TARGET="solution"; python -m pytest -q; Remove-Item Env:LAB_TARGET`. The variable persists
until it is removed, so always remove it, or later runs will silently grade the solution. Run pytest from this lab's
folder.

Until you finish, failing tests stop with `NotImplementedError`. `tests/test_relay.py`, `tests/test_data.py` and the
golden tests of provided reports pass from the start.

## Sample of acceptable evidence (excerpt)

> Over 30 days the static rule paged 732 times, 644 of them for nothing; the workbook policy paged twice, both for
> real outages, and reset 29 minutes after E1 against 56 for a single window. The slow burn E2 never paged and opened
> a ticket after 458 minutes, which is the right speed for an event spending a third of the budget over a day and a
> half. On 2 February the latency objective paged at 15:16, ten minutes after the attack began; the event spent 49.3%
> of the latency budget, so the budget policy asks for a postmortem and a release-risk review.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause |
| --- | --- |
| `window_sums` is off by one | The window ends with minute i inclusive: prefix[i + 1] - prefix[max(0, i + 1 - minutes)]. |
| Burn-rate episodes start a minute early | Both windows must reach the rate; check the short window too. |
| A row fires in a quiet minute | The long window needs at least max(1, min_events) events. |
| The golden alerts test differs from Relay's | Page episodes and ticket episodes are separate; sort all by (start, action). |
| Reset minutes are positive when you expect negative | Reset is the last matching episode's end minus the event's end. |
| `test_generator_reproduces_committed_data` fails | Data edited by hand; regenerate with `python scripts/generate_data.py`. |
| `ModuleNotFoundError: relay` | Run from `starter/` or `solution/`; importing `slolab` puts `relay-repo/src` on the path. |

## Known limits

- The monitor command runs and is tested, but the cloud deployment is not live: the task definitions need the
  collector sidecar, the gateway and monitor, the log groups and the pager's secret, and the environment carries
  no traffic until the PostgreSQL adapter lands. The monitor cannot trip a kill switch yet (no shared registry),
  the worker does not yet export its own telemetry, and the weekly RF-001 job is not scheduled (ADR-038).
- The GenAI semantic conventions are in Development status; `semconv.py` names the version it follows.
- The redactor's known misses are pinned, not fixed. Free text will always hold things no pattern finds.
