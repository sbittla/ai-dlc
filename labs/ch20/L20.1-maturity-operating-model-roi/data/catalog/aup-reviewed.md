# Acceptable use of AI tools (aup-v1.1)

Applies from 26 February 2027 (v1) and 15 June 2027 (v1.1) to everyone who writes, reviews or operates the company's
software. Catalog: 2027.06.1.

## 1. Scope

This policy covers every AI tool used for company work: coding agents, chat assistants, completion, tool servers and
agents that act without a person approving each step. It covers personal devices and personal accounts used for work.

## 2. Approved tools

Use only tools whose catalog status is approved, or trial within the trial's conditions. The catalog names each tool's
highest data class, its conditions and its owner. Ask for a new tool through the catalog request; security reviews it
within ten working days.

## 3. Data rules

Never put Restricted data into a tool outside the company's own accounts. Never paste a secret, a token, a customer's
personal data or a production log into any chat. Remove what you can before you share it, and use the approved tool's
paste filter as a backstop, not a guarantee.

## 4. Prohibited uses

Personal accounts of consumer AI tools for company work. Letting an agent approve, merge or deploy its own change.
Running an autonomous agent without an approved safety and security case.

## 5. Your responsibilities

You are accountable for what you merge, whoever or whatever wrote it. Say in each pull request what an agent produced
and how you checked it.

## 6. Reporting and amnesty

Report a mistake, a leak or use of an unapproved tool to security@ or in the AI channel. Reports made in good faith
are handled without blame; from 26 February to 12 March 2027, past use of unapproved tools may be reported with no
consequence beyond rotating what was exposed.

## 7. Training

Complete the AI tools security course before using a coding agent, and a refresher every twelve months.
