## What the canary saw

For each judged minute that was not "ok", the signal, the canary's and the stable version's bad shares and burn
rates. Was the decision right, and would a person watching graphs have made it sooner or later?

## What all at once would have done

Bad answers served, minutes until the bad version stopped serving, and whether a healthy release would have been
rolled back.

## The policy

Would you change a threshold, a bake time or the minimum number of requests? Say which scenario's result would
move, in which direction, and what it costs.
